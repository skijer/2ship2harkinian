# OoT Items in MM

The six items appear in Give/Remove and share cells on the main Items page. Slingshot shares Powder Keg, Boomerang shares Magic Beans, and Megaton Hammer shares Pictograph Box. The three spells use their original OoT cell positions. Registered items require human Link.

- Fairy Slingshot: hold its assigned button to load a seed and release to fire. A/B/C-Up cancels. Starts with 30 seeds. Bullet Bags raise capacity to 40 or 50, and the infinite bag stops consumption.
- Boomerang: hold its assigned button to aim and release to throw. OoT's En_Boom flight: a swept quad hits enemies with Zora boomerang damage, grabs drops and Skulltula tokens, bounces off walls and returns after 20 frames. Z-targeted throws curve toward the target.
- Megaton Hammer: press its assigned button to swing. Held with OoT's hand-and-hammer display list (MM fist patched in). Neutral or forward/back stick swings overhead, left/right swings sideways, with OoT's active frames and recovery animations. The overhead swing smashes the floor on frame 7 (quake, shockwave, Goron-pound impact); hitting a wall rebounds. With OoT Item Upgrades, the second Hammer becomes Gerudo Axe.
- Din's Fire (12 MP): OoT's expanding flame sphere, red screen tint and pull-back camera. Enemies are released from the cast freeze on frame 30 so the dome can hit them.
- Farore's Wind (12 MP, dungeons only): sets the warp point at the last room respawn; the green marker flies there and lights the room. Cast again to return, dispel or exit. Returning warps in from above and consumes the point.
- Nayru's Love (24 MP): orb, then the blue diamond for 1,200 frames. Link is protected without flashing. The magic meter stays locked while it lasts, and the shield carries across scene changes.

Randomizer → Mods → Slingshot Infinite Upgrades offers Off, Progressive and Condensed Progressive. This port uses the four-copy OoT pool as its baseline. Progressive adds a fifth copy for the infinite upgrade. Condensed makes the second copy infinite. The 5- and 30-seed refills are available as `oot.deku_seeds_5` and `oot.deku_seeds_30` for console drops.
