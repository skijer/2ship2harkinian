# OoT Item Upgrades for MM

- `oot_items` Hammer → Gerudo Axe: a custom weapon that is thrown and breaks silver rocks through MM's routines and drops.
- Kokiri → Razor → Gilded: MM's native sword progression.
- `oot_equipment` Master Sword → Real Master Sword: beams at full health.
- Giant's Knife → Biggoron → Great Fairy's Sword: a knife that lasts eight hits, a custom sword, and MM's native sword with health and magic recovery.
- Hookshot → Longshot → Ultrashot: one native slot, with double range at level two and quadruple range and more speed at level three.

Progressive upgrades are available in the Save Editor. Randomizer simulations never change the real equipment. Needs `oot_items`, `oot_equipment` and the OoT Asset Loader.
