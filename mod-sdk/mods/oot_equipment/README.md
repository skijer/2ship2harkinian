# OoT Equipment

Seven ownership-only pieces: Master Sword, Biggoron Sword, Deku Shield, OoT Mirror Shield, Goron Tunic, Iron Boots and Hover Boots. Give/Remove in Save Editor > Mod Items & Equipment changes their saved ownership; owned pieces appear on the equipment page over Masks. Icons and English pause names come from OoT Asset Loader. No combat, shield, tunic or boot behavior is implemented yet, and these pieces cannot be worn or assigned to buttons. Kokiri Sword and Hylian Shield are represented by MM's existing equipment.
